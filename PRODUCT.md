# AO Pilot Dashboard

## Register

product

## Users

The workstation owner supervising AO-managed coding sessions, including CIE workers.

## Product Purpose

Replace the legacy browser Dashboard without depending on its repository. Preserve project/session navigation, session detail, and the interactive terminal.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, precise, restrained. Preserve the existing workflow rather than redesigning the product.

## Anti-references

Do not introduce a marketing landing page, decorative metrics, or a new workflow. Do not show indefinite Connecting when the daemon or terminal has failed.

## Design Principles

- Keep project, session, branch, and terminal identity visible.
- Distinguish connection health from agent activity.
- Never infer migration or task completion from an idle terminal.
- Keep destructive and successor actions out of automatic page behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled controls, non-color-only status, readable contrast, and reduced motion.
